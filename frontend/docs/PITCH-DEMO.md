## Why it is creative (for Q&A)
- **The role flip:** the user doesn't plan; tasks have to justify themselves, and the default is *not* doing them.
- **Interaction, not a form:** cross-examining, pardoning, appealing and pacts are ways to negotiate with your own day.
- **Pacts:** tasks can form alliances. Two weak cases become one strong one, which no planner does.
- **Honest AI:** language models give the voices; a deterministic court gives the verdicts. Evidence is only what the user confirms.

## If something fails
- **"The court has restarted"** → click **Reconvene** (same case, same result).
- **No backend** → use the replay case or the backup video; the replay verdicts are not identical to the live ones, so narrate what is on screen.
- **A pause during cross-examination** (the model takes 5–10 s) → fill it: "The witness is thinking. So is the AI."
